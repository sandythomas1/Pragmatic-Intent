## Runtime on the real dev set (AC-007-16, partial)

The predictions here are **synthetic**, generated by a hash-seeded rule, not by any model. Only
the runtime and the Monte Carlo spread are evidence. The script lives in Claude's scratchpad and
is not committed; it prints counts only.

- **Data:** the dev evaluation set, 11,394 examples in 999 dialogues.
- **Grid:** 47 runs.
  - Stage 1: `deberta-v3-large` and `roberta-large`, each 3 training conditions × 5 seeds;
    `tfidf-context`, 1 run.
  - Stage 2: a matched `hybrid`, 3 training conditions × 5 seeds; an untrained `llm`.
- **Config:** the committed `analysis-v1.toml`, B = 10,000.
- **Result:**
  - the component check passed for all 47 runs;
  - 1,632 statistics, none sparse or unstable;
  - **314 s** on a laptop CPU, against the 15-minute bound;
  - two-seed Monte Carlo agreement of the primary-design interval endpoints was at most
    **0.0008** across the 5 systems (tolerance ±0.002), so B = 10,000 suffices at this size;
  - the smallest L0–L2 cell had 731 dialogues.
