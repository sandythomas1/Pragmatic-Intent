How do conversational context and linguistic ambiguity interact when machine learning and language models infer a user's underlying intent?

This is a draft research specification, not completed research. The data join, form rubric, weak-label trainers and dev scorers exist. Literary collection, contextual gold, Stage 2 scoring and the final test protocol still need work.

02

Two sources of evidence. Separate claims.

The professor's literary approach becomes a real collection track, with the original assistant-intent question guiding what qualifies.

Primary December analysis

Assistant dialogue

Collect original MultiWOZ user turns with their actual preceding conversation. Validate contextual request function and intended meaning.

  • Preserve official dialogue splits.
  • Exclude whole previously inspected test dialogues.
  • Keep DIRECT rewrites as explicit auxiliary weak supervision.

Goal: 160–240 test items across at least 50 groups. Smaller fallback: 120 across at least 30. Counts are proposed, not collected.

Held-out transfer / literary pilot

Addressed literary exchanges

Screen fables, parables and other human-authored works for a speaker, an addressee and textual evidence for a request.

  • Pin the edition, translation and locator.
  • Retain the original exchange; no invented assistant context.
  • Group retellings and keep test stories out of training/prompts.

Goal: 20–40 items across at least 10 story families. Morals, motives and punchlines do not automatically qualify as requests.

Form is the wording

L0 is direct, L1 conventionally indirect, L2 a strong hint, L3 a mild hint. Do not define a level by how much context it needs.

Function is what the speaker does

A statement may inform, decline or request. A wording-only “informing” label can still have request function in context.

Ambiguity is a separate measurement

Independent readers may support different interpretations. Disagreement is a proxy; it is not proof of inherent ambiguity.

Depth is an exploratory evidence chain

One versus multiple interpretive links is annotated separately. Story length and indirectness are not depth measures.

Main-input rule: no LLM-generated utterances, invented contexts, authored request ladders or fabricated contrastive twins. Human-written gold paraphrases are annotations, kept out of model input.

Already existsDIRECT/MultiWOZ join, rubric v2, calibration rounds, weak TF-IDF/encoder trainers, dev scoring and fact-drift audit.
Needs extensionLiterary ingestion, contextual annotation records, source-aware rendering, Stage 2 scorer, LLM/hybrid adapters and frozen test analysis.
03

Change the context. Keep the words.

Context ablation changes what information a model receives. It does not rewrite the target or create more independent examples.

A teaching example

Invented illustration · not study data

All exchanges below are written solely to explain the experiment. They are excluded from the research corpus; no model prediction or empirical score is displayed.

Target utterance · unchanged in every view

“It's getting cold in here.”

Stage 1 · Detect

Is there a request? If its form is known, is it direct or indirect? Genuine non-requests are essential for measuring over-inference.

Stage 2 · Interpret

What action or information is intended? Score supported categories and required slots. Evaluate all gold indirect requests as well as the routed hybrid.

H1Does performance fall as requests become less explicit?
H2 · primaryDoes relevant context help more at higher indirectness levels? Overall improvement alone cannot answer this.
H3Does the context benefit differ across the compared model families?
H4Does detecting the request degrade less than recovering its meaning, on the same items?
H5Which supported intent categories are harder? Treat small cells as exploratory.
